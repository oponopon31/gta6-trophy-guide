# Leonida Atlas — GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker

The complete guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI — entirely in your browser, 100% offline, no upload. Every trophy with its official name, description and grade, a checklist saved in your browser with export and import, filters by kind and grade, a spoiler setting, a roadmap to the platinum, and a guide to each trophy in our own words that cites its sources.

## What makes it an atlas

- **Audit bar** — the top of the page shows the audit at a glance: 55/55 trophies verified, 188 steps cited, 2 unconfirmed, 1 missable. Every number is computed live from the data, never typed by hand.
- **Step-level citations** — every single step of every guide carries its own source tag: [Exophase], [PSNProfiles], [PowerPyx], or [Official description] for advice inferred from the official description. 188 cited steps in total.
- **Atlas presentation** — a paper-atlas look with a compass rose, serif display type, medal swatches per grade, and a sticky platinum progress bar with a "Next up" pointer.
- **Step counts that count** — 23 trophies have a numeric stepper (− / + buttons, saved in your browser); reaching the target auto-marks the trophy earned.

## The data

55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation until earned. Names, descriptions and grades are PlayStation's, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles (the two agree on all of them).

- **Confirmed:** Ten Pin (motorbike/bicycle, kick 10 pedestrians with Square in 60s, Vice City / Ocean Beach) and The Truth Is Out There (road NW of Starlet Motel, Kelly County, after 02:00, solo, headlights flashing) — both confirmed by PowerPyx.
- **Unconfirmed (2):** In The Beginning… (id 44) and Bad Luck, I Guess (id 49, believed related to the Payday Loans store from the Prologue) — marked as such until a source pins down their trigger.
- **Missable (1):** Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) — meditate at each spot as soon as you find it.

## Roadmap to the platinum

1. **Play the story** — chapters in order, plus the five side-mission strands. Earns 12 story trophies plus the strand finales.
2. **Activities, collectibles & counts** — 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 drone-race wins, 20 race wins, 8 stranger strands, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK It spots, 50 challenges, 100 vehicles, 5 bank vaults, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 18 meditation spots, and the money goals. Earns A Long Road and the majority of the rest.
3. **Clean-up & platinum** — the two unconfirmed trophies once confirmed, the UFO and Ten Pin, and 100% completion. Earns the platinum, Vice Got a Price!

## Use

Serve it locally with `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. Or run `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` to build the hand-in (app/ + screenshots/ + dossier.json).

Progress saves in localStorage; export it to a file and import it back. The spoiler setting hides story trophies' names and descriptions until they are earned.

## Sources

The official list on Exophase for each trophy's name, description and grade; PSNProfiles for PSN's tracked counts; PowerPyx for the two confirmed methods and the two unconfirmed notes; our own advice from the official description where a step is inferred, marked as such. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used.
