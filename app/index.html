    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours at difficulty 4/10. One playthrough is enough, there are no online trophies, and free-roam continues after the story — but there is no mission replay.

    1. Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

      Finish the five chapters and the named missions as they come. Take the optional missions and character strands as they appear, and scan vehicles and study animals on the way — progress already counts toward the 100% checklist.

      Earns: 12 story trophies — Chapters 1–4, the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the final mission.

    2. Step 2 — Clean up Leonida about 100 hours

      After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through what is left: collectibles, counters, activities, robberies and the challenges. The checklist is lenient — 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals and 70 of 80 challenges are enough.

      Earns: A Long Road (100% completion) and the majority of the rest — collectibles, activities, counters and robberies.

    3. Step 3 — Platinum minutes

      Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands and the character quest finals, then check the list for anything left. When the last other trophy pops, the platinum follows at once.

      Earns: Vice Got a Price! — the platinum trophy.

    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

    Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in all. The first meditation is offered in Chapter 1: after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditating at Jason's house in the morning — follow her to the balcony and accept. Finish that balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1; it cannot be done later. The other 17 spots are found across Leonida.
