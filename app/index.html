Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough, difficulty 4/10

Figures from PowerPyx’s GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap: 55 trophies, all offline, 0 online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals, no mission replay — but free-roam continues after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for a trophy run.

  1. Step 1 — Finish the story 60–80 hours

    Play the 5 chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go — progress toward the 100% checklist already counts during the story, even though the checklist itself only becomes visible afterwards. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see the missable box below) and keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.

    Earns: all 12 story trophies, plus the first meditation toward Positive Mental Attitude.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% Completion Checklist ~100 hours

    After the story, work through the checklist. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: any 200 of 277 base-model vehicles, any 100 of 255 animals, any 70 of 80 challenges, any 100 optional missions. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need to be done in full. This step earns A Long Road (gold) and most of the remaining trophies on its own.

    Earns: A Long Road (gold) and most of the remaining trophies — stunts, challenges, robberies, collections.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

    Strangers & Freaks strands are not part of 100%, so finish the 8 counting strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete), then mop up the rest. The platinum pops when the last trophy does.

    Earns: the last Strangers & Freaks strands and the platinum itself — Vice Got a Price!

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

Exactly one trophy can be missed — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida). The first meditation happens at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 and never comes back: after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she is going to meditate and walks to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. PowerPyx notes it is still unclear whether that first meditation is required for the trophy, since it is not marked as a Meditation Spot like the rest — do it anyway. Safety net: the game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave; save manually at the start of Chapter 1 and again right after the balcony meditation, and keep a save at the start and end of every chapter. The other 54 trophies are all earnable in free-roam after the story.

Source: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (Step 1 note and Positive Mental Attitude entry); cross-checked with GTABase and Beebom’s meditation-spot guide.

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 trophy list as published on PSN, via Exophase and PSNProfiles (18 Nov 2026) — the two trackers agree on all 55.
