    Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

    Built from the official descriptions (chapters, missions, collectibles and counts). Difficulty 4/10, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave).

    1. Step 1 — Story + side content as it appears (60–80 h). Play Chapters 1–5 in order; every story trophy pops automatically. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends (the only possibly missable trophy). Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals with binoculars as you go — 100% progress already counts during the story. Do optional missions when they appear; quest givers who vanish return later.
    2. Step 2 — 100% Completion checklist + 70 of 80 Challenges (~100 h). After the story the checklist unlocks under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Thresholds are lenient (~60% true completion): 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, any 100 optional missions, any 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all 15 Target Decks, 15 of 18 meditations. Most of this fills itself while you chase the other trophies.
    3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and misc cleanup (~10 h). Strangers & Freaks are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 counting strands, the remaining side questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), the money trophies ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations) and any stragglers. Then the Platinum pops.

    Missable trophies — 1 possible, 54 safe

    Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free-roam. There is no mission replay, so the safety net is manual saves, not replaying missions.

    TrophyWhat can be missedHow to stay safe
    Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze)One meditation at Jason's house exists only in Chapter 1. After the mission Down to Earth, Lucia meditates on the balcony in the morning; it is unconfirmed whether it counts toward the 18 spots, so it may be required.After Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. Keep a manual save at the start of Chapter 1.
    Everything elseNothing. Optional missions that disappear, fences that leave, and quest givers who go temporarily unavailable do not affect any trophy — all trophy-related characters and activities return or have spares.Play the story normally; do side content when it appears, but do not panic about expiring icons.
