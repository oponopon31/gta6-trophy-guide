Road to the Platinum

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots before the story ends.

  1. Step 1 — Story first

    Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Earns: 12 story trophies + Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked, Snow Storm and the chapter trophies.

  2. Step 2 — People & places

    Finish the character finals (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the 8 stranger strands, and explore all 52 areas. Earns: A Long Road's backbone + the majority of the rest.

  3. Step 3 — Cleanup & platinum

    Grind the steppers (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK spots, 42 gold medals), finish the collectibles, then the last trophy pops the platinum.

Earns: step 1 — 12 story trophies · step 2 — A Long Road + the majority of the rest · step 3 — the platinum.

Atlas of Leonida

The state, at a glance. Every region below holds trophies — follow the platinum route in order.

  • Vice City — story, Ten Pin, meditation spots, races
  • Kelly County — the UFO (#50), Starlet Motel, countryside strands
  • Leonida Keys — cards, WAiNK spots, fishing with Cal
  • Ambar — hunting, exploration, stranger strands
  • Ocean Beach — Ten Pin, In The Beginning… (#44, unconfirmed)
