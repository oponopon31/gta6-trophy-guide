Roadmap to the platinum

Three steps, in order. Built from the official descriptions.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story

    Play through the story in order, chapter by chapter, and finish the side-mission strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone as they open.

    Earns: 12 story trophies (chapters, the final mission, the named story missions) plus the five strand finales.

  2. Step 2 — Activities, collectibles & counts

    Work the counted activities and collectibles: 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range golds, 14 drone-race wins, 20 race wins, 8 stranger strands, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK It spots, 50 challenges, 100 vehicles, 5 bank vaults, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 18 meditation spots, plus the money goals ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 8 garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations).

    Earns: A Long Road and the majority of the remaining trophies.

  3. Step 3 — Clean-up & platinum

    Mop up whatever is left — the two unconfirmed trophies once their triggers are pinned down, the UFO and Ten Pin, and 100% completion.

    Earns: the platinum, Vice Got a Price!

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

Meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. A spot can be missed if its area becomes unavailable, so meditate at each spot as soon as you find it.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy in the list can possibly be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story.
