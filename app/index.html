Roadmap to the platinum

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots before the final mission. Spots may become unreachable after the story ends.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story

    Finish Gone Fishing, Chapters 1–4 and the final mission, plus the named story missions.

    Earns: 12 story trophies (ids 2–13).

  2. Step 2 — Sweep the world

    Complete the 8 stranger strands, collect the card sets, explore all 52 areas, try every activity, and work the counters (stunts, races, vehicles, challenges).

    Earns: A Long Road + the majority of the rest — stranger finals, collectibles, activities and counters.

  3. Step 3 — Clean up and platinum

    Finish the two confirmed secrets (Ten Pin, the UFO), the grind counters (250 stunts, $250,000 laundering, 500 choices), and the two unconfirmed trophies if they are verified.

    Earns: the platinum — Vice Got a Price!
